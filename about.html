About us

Building Ethiopia's most trusted B2B sales bench.

We exist to make Ethiopian sales talent easier to trust, hire, and scale — and to give ambitious professionals a real path into sales careers.

Our mission

Close the gap between great potential and real opportunity.

Ethiopia has no shortage of ambitious, capable people who could thrive in B2B sales. What's been missing is the structure that turns that potential into reliable performance — and the trust that lets companies hire with confidence.

Meseret Salita is that structure. We train and vet talent, match them with companies, and stay involved after the hire — so both sides win.

What we stand for

The principles behind every placement.

Trust first

We only introduce people we'd vouch for. Reputation is the business.

A real bar

We hold a high standard for readiness — and don't lower it for speed.

Both sides matter

A placement only works if it's right for the company and the person.

We stay involved

Support doesn't end at the offer. We're there after the hire, too.

Why Ethiopia

A fast-growing pool of business-minded talent.

With the right preparation, Ethiopian professionals bring motivation, communication, and commercial discipline that B2B teams value.

  • Large, motivated talent basePeople who choose sales as a career and want to be developed.
  • English-capable communicationScreened for clear, professional written and spoken English.
  • Time-zone friendlyComfortable overlap with EMEA and much of the US workday.
  • Cost-effective, high standardCompetitive engagement models without cutting corners.
