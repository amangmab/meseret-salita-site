The problem

Good sales hires are slow to find and expensive to get wrong.

Screening CVs, running interviews, and onboarding someone who turns out not to fit can cost months. Meseret Salita removes that risk: we train and vet first, introduce only people who are ready, and stay involved so the placement actually works.

You hold the bar. We do the heavy lifting to meet it.

What you get

Trained. Vetted. Supported.

Three commitments behind every introduction we make.

Trained for B2B execution

Prospecting, discovery, qualification frameworks, CRM hygiene, follow-up discipline, and professional business communication — practiced before they reach you.

We assess readiness, professionalism, communication quality, and role fit — so the people you meet are genuinely ready, not just available.

How engagement works

A simple path from brief to working hire.

Most companies move from first call to a shortlist in about two weeks.

See the full process

  1. Step 1

    Share your brief

    A short call to understand your sales motion, target market, role, and what "great" looks like for you.

  2. Step 2

    Meet a shortlist

    We introduce a small set of pre-vetted candidates aligned to your role — no CV pile to wade through.

  3. Step 3

    Select & onboard

    You interview, choose, and we help with a smooth start so your hire is productive sooner.

  4. Step 4

    Ongoing support

    We stay involved to support performance — and use replacement flexibility if the fit isn't right.

Replacement flexibility, built in

If a placement isn't working out — as a guide, within the first [30 days] — we introduce a replacement at no additional placement fee, so a single mis-hire doesn't set your pipeline back.

Common questions.

How quickly can we hire?

For common roles, expect a shortlist within roughly two weeks of your brief. More senior or specialised roles can take longer.

What if a placement doesn't work out?

Replacement flexibility is part of how we work. If the fit is wrong, we help you transition to another vetted candidate.
