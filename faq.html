FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything companies and candidates ask most often. Can't find your answer? Get in touch.

For companies

Hiring sales talent.

How fast can we meet candidates?

For common roles, we typically share a shortlist within about two weeks of your brief. More senior or specialised roles can take longer.

Which sales roles do you place?

SDRs, BDRs, Account Executives, Sales Operations, and Customer Growth roles. If you need a role we don't list, tell us and we'll tailor the search. See the catalog →

How do you vet candidates?

We assess sales fundamentals, communication quality, professionalism, and role fit before any introduction — so the people you meet are genuinely ready, not just available. See the process →

How are you different from a typical offshore SDR agency?

We're not a call centre renting out seats. We train and vet each person to a real B2B bar before you meet them, introduce a small shortlist you choose from, and stay involved after the hire — with replacement flexibility if the fit isn't right. You get an accountable partner, not a black box.

What if a placement doesn't work out?

Replacement flexibility is built into how we work. As a guide, if a hire isn't working out within the first [30 days], we introduce another vetted candidate at no additional placement fee.

Are these employees, contractors, or embedded?

Both options exist. With a Placement, the professional joins your company directly. With the Embedded model, they work as part of your team while we manage the day-to-day engagement. We'll help you choose the structure that fits your setup and any cross-border considerations. See pricing →

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the engagement model — placement, embedded, or build-a-bench. See pricing →

Do candidates work remotely?

Our talent is based in Ethiopia, with working hours that overlap comfortably with EMEA and much of the US workday.

For talent

Applying & training.

Who can apply?

Ambitious people who communicate clearly, are coachable, and want a real career in sales. See what we look for →

Do I need prior sales experience?

No. We look for drive, strong communication, and coachability. Our training builds the B2B fundamentals companies expect.

How long is the training?

We'll share the current program length and format when you apply.

Is there a cost to apply or train?

Applying is free, and we're transparent about any program details before you commit.

What roles could I be placed in?

SDR, BDR, Account Executive, Sales Operations, and Customer Growth roles, depending on your strengths and the opportunities available.

What if my placement doesn't work out?

We help you pursue another opportunity rather than starting from zero — and we stay in your corner.

How do I apply?

Use our contact form and select "Apply as talent," or email hello@meseretsalita.com.
