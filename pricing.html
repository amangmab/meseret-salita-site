Pricing

Three ways to hire. One standard of talent.

Choose the model that fits how you hire — a one-off placement, an embedded professional, or a whole bench. Replacement flexibility and ongoing support are included in every model.

Placement is a one-time fee for a direct hire. Embedded is a managed monthly model where we handle the ongoing relationship. Build-a-bench is for hiring several roles. In every model, the talent's salary or compensation is separate from our fee — we'll walk through the full picture on a call.

Pricing questions

Before you ask.

When do I pay?

It depends on the model. We'll walk you through terms on an intro call so there are no surprises.

Is there a replacement guarantee?

Yes — replacement flexibility is built into every model. As a guide, if a placement isn't working out within the first [30 days], we introduce a replacement at no additional placement fee. We'll confirm the exact terms before you commit.

Can I hire multiple roles?

Yes — the Build-a-bench model is designed for hiring several roles or scaling a sales team over time.
