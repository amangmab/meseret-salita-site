Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026.

1. Who we are

Meseret Salita ("we", "us", "our") connects companies with trained, vetted B2B sales talent from Ethiopia. You can contact us at hello@meseretsalita.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you provide: name, email, company, and message details when you contact us or apply.
  • Candidate information: details relevant to training, vetting, and placement, where you apply as talent.
  • Usage information: basic, privacy-respecting analytics about how the website is used.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide our services.
  • To train, vet, and match candidates with companies.
  • To improve our website and services.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing

We share candidate information with prospective employers only as needed to facilitate introductions and placements. We do not sell personal information. We may use trusted service providers (for example, email or form processing) who handle data on our behalf.

5. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described here or as required by law.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact hello@meseretsalita.com.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the updated version here with a revised date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@meseretsalita.com.
