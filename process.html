How it works

A clear placement process for both sides.

Four stages — Train, Vet, Match, Support — designed so companies hire with confidence and talent build careers on a real safety net.

  1. TrainWe prepare professionals for B2B sales expectations and day-to-day execution.
  2. VetWe screen for skill, professionalism, communication, and fit.
  3. MatchCompanies meet talent aligned with their role, market, and collaboration style.
  4. SupportWe stay involved after placement and help with changes when needed.

Stage 01 · Train

Preparation that builds real B2B habits.

Before anyone is introduced to a company, they build the fundamentals that B2B teams rely on — so they can contribute sooner.

For talent

Hands-on practice in prospecting, qualification, CRM habits, communication, and follow-up discipline.

For companies

Less ramp time. Candidates already understand the motions and language of B2B sales.

Stage 02 · Vet

A real bar — applied before you meet anyone.

We assess readiness through practical evaluation — written outreach, mock calls, and role-plays — not just a conversation. Only people who clear the bar get introduced.

  • Sales fundamentalsCan they prospect, qualify, and follow up with discipline?
  • Communication qualityClear, professional written and spoken English.
  • Professionalism & reliabilityDo they show up and follow through?
  • Role fitAre they aligned to the kind of role and motion you're hiring for?

Stage 03 · Match

The right person for your motion and market.

We introduce a focused shortlist aligned to your role, market, and the way your team works — so interviews are productive from the first call.

For companies

A focused shortlist — typically three to five relevant candidates, not a CV pile. You interview and choose.

For talent

Introductions to companies hiring for roles you've prepared for.

Stage 04 · Support

We don't disappear after the placement.

We stay involved to support performance and expectations — and use replacement flexibility if the fit isn't right.

For companies

Help aligning on expectations, and a clear path to change the person if needed.

For talent

Ongoing support and another opportunity if a placement doesn't work out.

Typical timing

For common roles, companies usually move from brief to shortlist in about two weeks. More senior or specialised roles can take longer.
