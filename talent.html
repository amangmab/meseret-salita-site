For applicants

A serious path into B2B sales careers with real companies.

We help ambitious professionals become more employable, access genuine opportunities, and stay supported — even if a role doesn't work out the first time.

Who this is for

Ambitious people who want a real career in sales.

You don't need years of experience — you need drive, strong communication, and a willingness to be coached. Whether you're starting out or levelling up, we help you build the fundamentals B2B companies actually look for.

Then we connect you with companies hiring for the roles you've prepared for.

What you gain

Preparation, opportunity, and a safety net.

Training before placement

Build stronger sales fundamentals and learn exactly what companies expect in B2B roles.

Access to opportunities

Get considered for active roles with companies that are specifically looking for sales talent.

Replacement opportunity

If a placement isn't a good fit, we help you pursue another opportunity rather than starting from zero.

The training

What you'll learn.

Practical, B2B-focused fundamentals — the habits and skills hiring teams look for from day one.

Apply to join

  • Prospecting & researchFinding the right accounts and people, and building targeted outreach lists.
  • Outreach that gets repliesWriting clear cold emails, making confident calls, and following up with discipline.
  • Discovery & qualificationAsking strong questions and using simple frameworks to qualify opportunities.
  • CRM & pipeline habitsKeeping records clean, updating stages, and managing a pipeline professionally.
  • Business communicationProfessional written and spoken English, and representing a brand well.
  • Objection handling & follow-throughStaying calm under pushback and moving deals forward without being pushy.
